**Account Security** shows every device signed in to your account and lets you sign them all out. Open it from the profile menu.

## Your email

Shown at the top for reference. It can't be changed from the app.

## Change your password

Under **Change Password**, enter a **New Password** and **Confirm New Password** (at least 6 characters), then click **Change Password**. You're then asked whether to **Logout All Devices**. See [Password Reset and Account Access](/getting-started/onboarding-first-steps/password-reset).

## Devices & Activity

A list of every device signed in to your account. Each shows:

- The device, browser and operating system
- Location, when known
- **Last active** time
- **Active** (used in the last 10 minutes) or **Inactive**

Sessions that have been inactive for more than 7 days can be removed with the trash icon.

<Tip>
  See a device you don't recognize? Click **Logout All Devices**, then change your password and turn on [two-factor authentication](/account-and-security/two-factor-authentication).
</Tip>

## Logout All Devices

Signs you out everywhere, **including this browser**. Use it if you've signed in on a shared computer or lost a device. After confirming, sign in again.
