**Convert to Video** turns one of your images into a short video clip: the model turns, walks, or the camera moves around the product.

<Note>
  Convert to Video is available on paid plans.
</Note>

## Create a video

1. Open an image in the viewer, hover over it, and click **Convert to Video** in the preset row.
2. **Crop start frame** opens first. Choose **16:9** (horizontal) or **9:16** (vertical), adjust the zoom, and click **Apply crop**. You can change it later with **Adjust crop**. The **Image to Video** dialog then opens.

<Frame caption="The Image to Video dialog. Scroll down for Duration and Generate Video.">
  <img src="/images/viewer-convert-video.webp" alt="Image to Video dialog with start frame, optional end frame, prompt presets and the Model menu" />
</Frame>
3. **End frame (optional):** pick another image from **Existing images**, **Upload image**, or click **use same as start frame**. The video moves from the start frame to the end frame.
4. **Prompt:** choose a motion from **Browse** (filter by **Male**, **Female** or **All**), or describe it on the **Text** tab.
5. **Model:** **Fast** (default), **Lite**, **Quality** or **Omni Flash**.
6. **Duration:** **4 seconds** (default), **6 seconds** or **8 seconds**.
7. Click **Generate Video**.

You see **Video generation started. We'll keep you posted!** A **Creating Magic...** card appears, and you get a **Video Ready!** notification when it's done. Most videos take a few minutes.

<Tip>
  Make one 4-second **Fast** video to check the motion. Once you like it, make the final with **Quality** or a longer duration.
</Tip>

## Writing a good motion prompt

The start frame already sets the look, so describe **movement**, not the scene:

- Good: "Slow push-in as the model turns toward the camera, hair moving in a light breeze"
- Less good: "A woman in a red kurta in a garden" (this just repeats the image)

Keep it to one or two movements for a short clip.

## Troubleshooting

1. **"Video generation failed. Please try again or contact support."**
   **✅ Solution:** Try again. If it fails twice, try a simpler prompt or a different model.

2. **"You're out of credits to generate videos."**
   **✅ Solution:** Top up or upgrade. See [How Credits Work](/subscription-and-billing/credits-explained).

3. **"Convert to video is not included in your current plan."**
   **✅ Solution:** Contact us to add video to your plan.
