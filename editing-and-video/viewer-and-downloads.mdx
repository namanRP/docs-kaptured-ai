Every generated image can be opened full screen, compared with your original product photo, and downloaded in several formats.

## The Generations section

Under the studio steps, **Generations (N)** shows everything made for the selected product.

- Each card has an engine tag (for example **v1 - 2K**) so you know how it was made.
- **Compare with product image** opens your product photo and the result side by side.
- **Refresh** reloads the list.
- **Clear All** clears the list from view.
- **Edit Your Own Image** lets you upload any image and edit it with the same tools.

## The image viewer

Click an image to open it full screen.

<Frame caption="The image viewer toolbar: Crop image (1), Magic edit with AI (2) and Download (3).">
  <img src="/images/viewer-toolbar.webp" alt="Full-screen image viewer with the crop, Magic Edit, download and close buttons" />
</Frame>

From here you can:

- Move between images with the arrows. The counter shows where you are, for example **12 / 12**.
- **Crop image** (1), including exact sizes for Amazon, Etsy, Shopify and ads. See [Marketplace Crops](/editing-and-video/marketplace-crops).
- **Magic edit with AI** (2) to add your logo and ad text. See [Magic Edit](/editing-and-video/magic-edit).
- Hover over the image to show the editing tools: presets, a prompt box and **Convert to Video**. See [Editing Images with AI](/editing-and-video/editing-images).
- **Download** (3).

## Download options

<Frame caption="The download menu in the viewer.">
  <img src="/images/viewer-download-menu.webp" alt="Download menu listing Original, Compressed WebP, Enhanced and with-Logo options" />
</Frame>

| Option | What you get | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Original Image** | Full-resolution PNG | Printing, further editing |
| **Compressed WebP Image** | Much smaller file, same look | Websites and marketplaces with size limits |
| **Enhanced Image** | Sharpened, upscaled copy | Larger prints and zoomable product pages |
| **Enhance Image (Slow, but better)** | Higher-quality upscale that takes longer | Hero images |
| **Original Image with Logo** | Original plus your shop logo | Social media |
| **Enhanced Image with Logo** | Enhanced plus your shop logo | Social media at high resolution |

- **Enhance** runs in your browser and uses **no credits**. When it finishes, click **Compare results** to see the before and after.
- The **with Logo** options use the logo and **Logo Position** from [Shop Settings](/getting-started/platform-overview/shop-settings). If you haven't uploaded one you'll see "No shop logo configured. Set a logo in Shop Settings."
- Enhanced and logo downloads are on paid plans.

Files are named after the product, for example `silk-kurta-image-created-by-kaptured-ai.png`.

## Download several at once

Use the **Download** menu above **Generations**: **Download All**, **Download Selected**, or **Download Selected by Product**. Tick images with the checkbox on each card to select them. To download across many products, use the [Library](/library/bulk-actions).

<Warning>
  Generated images are deleted after **30 days**. Download anything you want to keep. See [Why Images Are Deleted After 30 Days](/library/auto-deletion).
</Warning>
