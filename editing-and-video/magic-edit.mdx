**Magic Edit** turns a generated photo into a finished marketing image: your logo, a headline, a call to action, laid out and ready to post. Save a layout as a **design** and you can apply it to any number of images later.

<Note>
  Magic Edit is available on paid plans.
</Note>

## Open Magic Edit

Open an image in the viewer and click **Magic edit with AI**. The **Magic Image Edit** dialog has three tabs:

<Frame caption="The Magic Image Edit dialog, on the Browse tab.">
  <img src="/images/viewer-magic-edit.webp" alt="Magic Image Edit dialog with Browse, Marketing and AI Edit tabs and layout options" />
</Frame>

- **Browse:** start from **Your Designs** or a simple layout: **Blank**, **Logo**, or **Logo with top-left label**.
- **Marketing:** **AI Ad Designs** that add headlines and calls to action, including A/B test sets (**Hook + CTA**, **Value Prop**, **Headline + Subheadline**) so you can try two versions of an ad.
- **AI Edit:** describe the design you want in words.

Pick a starting point and click **Start Magic Edit**. Adjust the text and layout, then click **Download final edit**.

## Save a design

Click **Save as design** to keep the layout. Saved designs appear under **Your Designs**. **Product names in a design update automatically** for each image you use it on, so one design works for your whole catalog.

To remove a saved design, delete it from **Your Designs**. This can't be undone.

## Apply a design to many images

In the [Library](/library/bulk-actions), select images and click **Magic Edit**. Pick a saved design and click **Apply & Download**. You get a ZIP with every image edited.
