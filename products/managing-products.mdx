**Products Management** lists every product in your shop, 12 per page. This article covers finding, editing and deleting products.

</Frame>

## Find a product

- **Search products...** searches by product name.
- **AI Search** (paid plans) finds products by description, by a photo, or both. Type something like "red embroidered kurta", upload a photo, or combine the two, then click **Search Products**. While it's on, you see **AI Search active**; click the X to clear it.

## What a product card shows

Each card shows the product's photos (use the dots to switch between them) and badges for price, **For**, categories, stone type, colors and sizes.

Hover a card for three buttons:

- **View image:** full-screen view of the photos, with download.
- **Edit product** (2): opens the product form. This is also where you add **extra details** (description, price, colors, sizes).
- **Delete product:** deletes it after you confirm.

## Start a photoshoot from a product

Click **Generate Images** (3) on the card. The AI Photo Studio opens with that product selected. See [Your First Photoshoot](/ai-photo-studio/your-first-photoshoot).

## Delete products

- **One product:** hover the card, click **Delete product**, then **Delete**.
- **Several products:** tick the checkbox on each card (or **Select all on page**), then click **Delete** in the toolbar.

<Warning>
  Deleting a product can't be undone. Images you already generated from it stay in the [Library](/library/library-overview) until they expire.
</Warning>

## Q: Can I import products from Shopify?

Not yet. **Import from Shopify** is coming in a future release. For now, use [Bulk Upload](/products/bulk-upload).

## Q: Why can't I add more products?

The Free plan allows 3 products. Upgrade to add more. See [Pricing Plans](/subscription-and-billing/pricing-plans).

## Q: Can I try a product without saving it?

Yes. In the AI Photo Studio, click **Add Temporary Product**. See [Your First Photoshoot](/ai-photo-studio/your-first-photoshoot#try-a-product-without-saving-it).
