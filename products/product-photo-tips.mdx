The AI can only reproduce what it can see. Better product photos mean fewer retries and fewer wasted credits. These tips apply to every shop type.

## The basics

- **Plain background.** A wall, a sheet or a flat surface works. If your background is busy, turn on **Erase background** when you crop.
- **Even light.** Daylight from a window is ideal. Avoid harsh shadows and colored light, which change how the color comes out.
- **The whole product in frame.** Don't cut off sleeves, hems or clasps. Use **Smart crop** to trim the extra space.
- **Sharp focus.** Blurry photos give blurry details.
- **True color.** Turn off beauty filters and heavy edits on your phone camera.

## Which views to add

You can add up to 4 photos per product. Pick them in this order:

1. **Front**: always the most important.
2. **Back**: if the back is different (a zip, a print, a deep neck).
3. **Front Close-up**: for embroidery, prints, stones or texture.
4. **Side** or **Pairing Piece**: for a side slit, or the dupatta or bottom that goes with the set.

<Tip>
  When you choose a pose from an angle you don't have a photo for, the studio suggests adding one, for example "consider adding a Back photo of the product". Add it for a more accurate result.
</Tip>

## Clothing

- Lay the garment flat or hang it straight. Folded or crumpled fabric confuses the fit.
- A photo on a mannequin or a person is fine; the AI replaces them.
- Set **For** correctly (Men, Women, Kids). It decides which models the garment fits on.

## Jewelry

- Photograph on a plain, non-reflective surface.
- Always set **Stone Type**; it makes a big difference to sparkle and color.
- Add a **Size Reference** view so earrings and necklaces come out at the right size on the model.

## Other products

- Show the product from the angle a customer would usually see it.
- Add a close-up if there's text, a label or fine texture that must stay readable.
