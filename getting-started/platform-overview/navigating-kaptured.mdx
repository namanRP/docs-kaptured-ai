Kaptured AI has a small number of pages, all reached from the menu in the header. This article explains what each one is for.

## The pages

| Page | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **AI Photo Studio** | Generate photos of your products on AI models, then edit them or turn them into videos. This is where most of your time goes. |
| **Library** | Every image and video you have generated, in one timeline. Search, filter, download in bulk, and apply Magic Edit designs to many images at once. |
| **Products Management** | Your product catalog. Add, edit and delete products and their photos. |
| **Style Customers** | Save your customers' photos and generate Virtual Try-On images of them wearing your products. Clothing shops only. |
| **Shop Settings** | Shop name, logo, category, logo position and which image engines you use. |
| **API Keys** | Add your own Google Gemini API key (optional). |
| **Billing & Plans** | Your plan, credit balance, top-ups, usage history and invoices. |
| **Account Security** | Password, two-factor authentication, and the devices signed in to your account. |

<Note>
  **Style Customers** and **Customers Management** only appear for clothing shops with **Enable Virtual Try-On Customers** turned on in [Shop Settings](/getting-started/platform-overview/shop-settings).
</Note>

## The header

The profile menu in the header links to every page above, plus **Logout**.

To check your credit balance, open **Billing & Plans**. The studio also tells you how many credits a generation will use before you confirm it.

## Where you land after signing in

- No products yet: you go to **Products Management** so you can add one. The AI Photo Studio needs at least one product.
- Otherwise: you go straight to the **AI Photo Studio**.
- If your plan has ended: you go to **Billing & Plans** to choose a plan. Your credits are kept. See [Payment Troubleshooting](/subscription-and-billing/payment-troubleshooting).

## How the pieces fit together

```text
Products Management → AI Photo Studio → Generations → Edit / Video / Download
        (add a product)   (pick model, background, pose)          ↓
                                                               Library
```

Everything you generate, wherever you generated it, shows up in the [Library](/library/library-overview).
