**Shop Settings** controls how your shop appears and how the AI Photo Studio behaves. Open it from the profile menu. Changes apply across the app as soon as you click **Save Changes**.

## Shop Details

<Frame caption="Shop Details: Upload Logo (1), Shop Category (2) and Logo Position (3).">
  <img src="/images/shop-settings.webp" alt="Shop Settings page showing shop name, logo upload, shop category and logo position" />
</Frame>

### Shop Name

Up to **14 characters**. The counter under the field shows how many you've used. A shop name is required.

### Shop Logo

Your logo is used for the **with Logo** download options.

1. Click **Upload Logo** (1) and pick an image (JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF or HEIC).
2. In **Crop Logo Image**, choose a **Mode**: **Square** (1:1) or **Freehand** (any shape). The smallest crop is 100×100 pixels.
3. Click **Confirm Crop & Upload**.

The logo is saved right away; you don't need to click **Save Changes**. It must be under 2 MB after we optimize it.

<Tip>
  Use a logo with a transparent background (PNG) so it sits cleanly on any photo.
</Tip>

### Shop Category

Choose the option that best describes what you sell (2). **This changes the steps in the AI Photo Studio**, so set it before you start.

| Category | Studio steps |
| --- | --- |
| **Apparel** (default) | Select Apparel, Select Model, Select Background, Select Pose, Select Accessories, Final Settings |
| **Jewelry** | Select Jewelry, Select Template, Select Model, Select Accessories, Final Settings |
| **Other Products** | Select Product, Select Model, Select Pose, Select Accessories, Final Settings |

For **Other Products**, pick a type: **Furniture**, **Food Product**, **Restaurant**, **Mobile** or **Custom** (type your own, for example "electronics").

### Logo Position

Where your logo goes on **with Logo** downloads (3): **Top Right** (default), **Top Left**, **Bottom Right** or **Bottom Left**.

### AI Generated Label

When on, adds an "AI generated" label to Virtual Try-On images of your customers. Off by default.

### Enable Virtual Try-On Customers

Apparel shops only. On by default. Turns on the **Style Customers** pages. See [Styling Customers with Virtual Try-On](/customers/styling-customers). Switching to any other category turns it off.

## Image engines

Choose which image engines can be used in your shop, and which one is picked by default.

<Frame caption="Image engines and Shop Overview, further down the page.">
  <img src="/images/shop-settings-engines.webp" alt="Image engines setting with the engines menu, primary engine and Save Changes button" />
</Frame>

- **Engines your team may use:** a grid of engines (**v1**, **v2**, **v3**) by resolution (**1K**, **2K**, **4K**). Each allowed cell shows its cost in credits per image. Click a row or column header to toggle the whole row or column. At least one engine always stays on.
- **Primary (default):** the engine that's preselected in the studio's **Final Settings**.

<Tip>
  Turn off engines you never want used, for example 4K, to avoid spending more credits than you meant to.
</Tip>

On the Free plan only **v1** is available. See [Final Settings and Image Engines](/ai-photo-studio/final-settings-and-image-engines) for how the engines differ.

## Shop Overview

Shows how many **Products** and **Customers** your shop has, with shortcuts to **Products Management** and **Customer Management**.
