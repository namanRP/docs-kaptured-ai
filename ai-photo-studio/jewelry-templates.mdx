Jewelry shops choose a **Template** instead of a background and pose. A template decides how the piece is shown: on a pedestal, in a display, worn by a model, hanging, or held in hands.

## Choose a template

In **Select Template**, filter by category:

| Category | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| **E-Commerce** | Clean catalog shots, for example **Studio Edge Display** |
| **On Surface** | The piece resting on a surface, like **Stone Pedestal Composition** |
| **In Retail Prop** | In a store display, like **Retail Display** |
| **Worn** | On a model, like **Worn Display** |
| **Suspended** | Hanging in the air, like **Suspended Display** |
| **In Hands** | Held in a model's hands, like **In Hands Display** |

Use **Search templates...** to find one by name, and **Choose a shade** to change the template's color.

A template is required. If you skip it you'll see "Template selection is required. Please select a template to continue."

## Add your own template

Click **Add Your Own Template** to upload a reference photo of a display style you like and reuse it.

## How templates affect the other steps

- Some templates don't include a model. The studio tells you if you pick a model with one of these ("The {template} template does not include a model face").
- Some templates don't allow surfaces or retail props in **Select Accessories**.

## Accessories for jewelry

The jewelry **Select Accessories** step has four tabs: **Objects**, **Surfaces**, **Retail Props** and **Lighting Aids**. You can upload your own objects and retail props, or **Describe via text** in up to 10 words. See [Accessories](/ai-photo-studio/accessories).

## Tips for jewelry

- Always set **Stone Type** on the product.
- Add a **Size Reference** view so pieces come out at the right size when worn.
- The **Jewelry** lens presets (**Best Overall**, **Luxury Look**, **Ecommerce**, **Jewelry on Model**) are tuned for shine and detail. See [Final Settings](/ai-photo-studio/final-settings-and-image-engines).
