The **Select Background** and **Select Pose** steps set the scene. Each has a library to browse, a way to save your own styles, and a **Text** tab to describe exactly what you want.

## Backgrounds

Background is a step for clothing shops. (Jewelry shops use [Templates](/ai-photo-studio/jewelry-templates); other products are placed in a setting that suits them.)

<Frame caption="Select Background: categories (1), Let AI Choose a Background with its color menu (2), Upload Background Image (3) and the Text tab (4).">
  <img src="/images/studio-background.webp" alt="Select Background step with category chips, AI choice, upload tile, saved backgrounds and shade swatches" />
</Frame>

### Browse

Pick a category (1): **All**, **Studio**, **Outdoor**, **Festive** or **Indoor**. Then click a backdrop. There are around 90, from "Grey studio" to outdoor streets and festive sets. Use the search button to find one by name.

**Let AI Choose a Background** (2) has a color option too, such as **White Studio**, **Black Studio**, **Warm Beige**, **Olive Green** or **Terracotta Pink**, for a clean studio color that suits your product. Pick an exact shade with the swatches under the list.

### Upload your own background

1. Click **Upload Background Image** (3) and crop it in **Crop Background Image**.
2. Tick **Save for later** to keep it. You can keep up to 10 saved backgrounds.

**Tip:** Use a photo of the empty scene. If there's a person in it, you'll see "For better results, remove the subject from the background image."

### My Styles

**Add Your Own Background Style** saves a background look you can reuse across products.

### Text

Switch to the **Text** tab (4) and describe the scene under **The background features**, for example "Soft morning light in a minimalist studio".

## Poses

<Frame caption="Select Pose: the view filters (1), the My Styles tab (2) and the Styling menu (3).">
  <img src="/images/studio-pose.webp" alt="Select Pose step with view filters, pose thumbnails, Browse, My Styles and Text tabs and the Styling menu" />
</Frame>

### Browse

Choose a category (**All**, **Male**, **Female**, **Kids**, **Toddler**) and a view (1): **Front**, **Front-Side**, **Side**, **Back-Side**, **Back-Face** or **Back**. Click a pose to select it. One pose is used per image.

<Tip>
  If you pick a view you have no product photo for, the studio suggests adding one, for example a **Back** photo for a back pose. It's worth doing: the AI can't see a back it has never been shown.
</Tip>

### My Styles

On the **My Styles** tab (2), click **Add Your Own Pose Style**, upload a reference photo of the pose you want, and click **Use Pose Style**. It's saved for next time.

### Text

Describe the pose in **Pose Description**, for example "Standing in slight contrapposto, left hand resting on the hip".

### Expression

On **My Styles** and **Text**, pick an expression: **Neutral**, **Smiling** or **Laughing**.

### Styling

The **Styling** menu (3) has options that depend on the garment: for example **Tucked out** or **Tucked in** for a shirt, **Pleated pallu** or **Floating pallu** for a saree, or which finger a ring is worn on.

## Favorites

Mark models, backgrounds, templates and styles as favorites and they move to the front of the list.
