**Multi Product Shoot** puts up to 3 of your products into one image, for example a top, a bottom and a jacket styled as one outfit.

## Create a group shot

1. In **Select Apparel** (or **Select Product**), choose the **Multi Product Shoot** tab.
2. Click 2 or 3 products. The counter shows how many are selected.
3. Wait for the preview to finish building. If you click generate too early you'll see "Collage is being generated, please wait".
4. Continue through the steps and generate as usual. The button reads **Generate N images with K products**.

## What's different in a group shot

To keep several products consistent, some choices are made by the AI:

| Step | In a group shot |
| --- | --- |
| Model | Must be **Let AI Choose**. Custom faces aren't allowed |
| Pose | You can pick a pose, or **Let AI Choose an Angle**. Custom poses aren't allowed |
| Accessories | Always picked by the AI |

## Troubleshooting

1. **"Please select at least 2 products for Group Shot"**
   **✅ Solution:** Select a second product, or switch back to **Single Product Shoot**.

2. **"Multiple product mode requires Let AI Choose model selection"**
   **✅ Solution:** In **Select Model**, click **Let AI Choose a Face**.

3. **One product is less accurate than the others.**
   **✅ Solution:** Check that product has a clear **Front** photo. See [Product Photo Tips](/products/product-photo-tips).
