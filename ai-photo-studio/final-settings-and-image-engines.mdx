**Final Settings** is the last step before you generate. It sets the camera look, the shape of the image, and which image engine makes it. The engine and resolution you pick set the credit cost.

<Frame caption="Final Settings: Focus (1), Lens (2), Aspect Ratio (3), Image Engine (4) and the generate button (5).">
  <img src="/images/studio-final-settings.webp" alt="Final Settings card with focus, lens, aspect ratio, image engine and the generate button" />
</Frame>

## Focus

Open the **Focus** menu (1):

- **Background Heavily Blurred** (default): the product and model stand out.
- **Strong Background Bokeh**: soft, round light spots in the background.
- **No Hair on Clothes**: keeps the model's hair off the garment so the product stays fully visible.

## Lens

Open the **Lens** menu (2):

- **Lens Preset:** for clothing, **Outdoor**, **Full Body**, **Studio** or **Creative**. For jewelry, **Best Overall**, **Luxury Look**, **Ecommerce** or **Jewelry on Model**.
- **Photo Effect:** a color and light style, such as **Clean E-Commerce Studio Look**, **Fashion Editorial Portra Look**, **Golden Hour Portrait**, **Sunset Backlight with Lens Flare** or **Fuji Classic Chrome Aesthetic**.

## Aspect ratio

Pick a shape (3): **1:1**, **9:16**, **16:9**, **4:5**, **5:4**, **3:2**, **2:3**, **4:3** or **3:4**. The studio remembers the last one you used.

| Use | Pick |
| --- | --- |
| Marketplace listings, Instagram feed | 1:1 or 4:5 |
| Stories, Reels, Shorts | 9:16 |
| Website banners | 16:9 |

The Free plan is limited to 1:1.

## Image engines

The **Image Engine** menu (4) lets you pick one or more engines, each at **1K**, **2K** or **4K**. **Each engine you select makes its own image**, so selecting two gives you two images to compare, and costs the sum of both. The generate button (5) updates to show how many images you'll get.

<Frame caption="The Image Engine menu: tick an engine at the resolution you want.">
  <img src="/images/studio-engine-menu.webp" alt="Image Engine menu open with v1, v2 and v3 at 1K, 2K and 4K" />
</Frame>

| Engine | Best for | 1K | 2K | 4K |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **v1** (Best Value, Standard quality) | Everyday catalog images, testing ideas | 12 | 20 | 35 |
| **v2** (Most Powerful, Pro quality) | Hero shots, fine details, jewelry close-ups | 40 | 50 | 90 |
| **v3** (Most Powerful, Pro quality) | Hero shots, a different look from v2 | 40 | 50 | 90 |

*Credits per image. Your account may have different rates; the confirm dialog always shows the exact cost.*

<Tip>
  Try an idea on **v1 at 1K** first. When you like the direction, generate the final on v2 or v3 at a higher resolution.
</Tip>

- Which engines appear depends on what's allowed in [Shop Settings → Image engines](/getting-started/platform-overview/shop-settings#image-engines). If you see "This image engine is not enabled for your shop", turn it on there.
- The Free plan includes **v1** only.
