The **Select Accessories** step completes the look. You can pick from our library, upload your own, or let the AI choose.

## Let the AI choose

**Let AI Choose Accessories** (2) picks items that suit the product and model. It's the quickest option.

## Clothing and other products

1. Filter by **Male**, **Female**, **Kids** or **All**.
2. Narrow down by type (1): **Footwear**, **Gear**, **Earrings**, **Necklaces**, **Bracelets** or **Lowers**.
3. Click the items you want. Each one has a color menu; leave it on **Let AI pick color** or choose a color.

You can also click **Describe via text** and name the accessory in a few words.

## Jewelry

Jewelry shops see four tabs:

- **Objects**: items placed near the piece
- **Surfaces**: what the piece rests on
- **Retail Props**: display stands and boxes
- **Lighting Aids**

You can also **Describe via text** in up to 10 words.

Some [templates](/ai-photo-studio/jewelry-templates) don't allow surfaces or retail props.

## Upload your own accessories

Click **Upload Accessory** (3) (or **Upload Object** / **Upload Retail Prop** for jewelry) to use your own shoes, bags, props and so on.

- Up to **5** of your own items per generation.
- Up to **10** saved items.
- Retail prop photos must be under 5 MB.

## Clear your picks

Click **Clear all** to start the step again.

## Group shots

In a [Multi Product Shoot](/ai-photo-studio/group-shots), accessories are always picked by the AI.
