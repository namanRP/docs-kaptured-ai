---

Generated images and videos are stored for **30 days** from the moment they're created, then deleted automatically. This keeps the app fast and your data footprint small.

## What is deleted

- Images and videos you generated in the AI Photo Studio, Library and Virtual Try-On, including edits.

## What is kept

- Your products and their photos.
- Your customers and their photos.
- Saved models, backgrounds, styles, templates and Magic Edit designs.
- Your credit balance and usage history.

## How to see what's expiring

In the last **7 days**, the Library shows a badge on each item: **Deletes in N days**, **Deletes in 1 day** or **Deleting soon**. Hover it to see the exact date.

## How to keep your images

Download them. The quickest way to save a lot at once:

1. Open the **Library**.
2. Set **Group** to **By week** or **By month**.
3. Tick the group header to select the whole period.
4. Click **Download → Originals**.

See [Bulk Download and Bulk Magic Edit](/library/bulk-actions).

<Tip>
  Make a habit of downloading your finals once a week, and store them wherever you keep your other brand assets.
</Tip>

## Q: Can I get back an image that was deleted?

No. Once deleted it can't be recovered. You can generate a similar image again from the same product and settings.

## Q: Can I keep images for longer?

Not at the moment. Download anything you want to keep.
