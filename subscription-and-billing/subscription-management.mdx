Everything about your subscription is on **Billing & Plans**. Payments are handled securely by Stripe.

## Your current plan

The **Current plan** card shows:

- **Plan**, for example "Brand Plan"
- **Credit balance**
- **Renews** (or **Cancels**) with the date
- A status badge: **Active**, **Canceling**, **Payment overdue** or **Expired**

## Upgrade

Click **Switch to {Plan}** on a bigger plan and confirm. You're charged the prorated difference for the rest of the billing period, and **the extra credits are added straight away**. For example, moving from Studio to Brand mid-month adds 2,000 credits.

You'll see "Plan updated." when it's done.

## Downgrade

Click **Switch to {Plan}** on a smaller plan and confirm. You keep the credits you already have this month; the smaller allowance applies from the next monthly refresh. Any price difference is prorated by Stripe.

## Switch between monthly and yearly

Use the **Yearly** / **Monthly** switch (3), then pick your plan.

## Update your card or download invoices

Click **Manage billing** (2). This opens the Stripe billing portal, where you can:

- Update your payment method
- See and download every invoice and receipt
- Change or cancel your plan

## Cancel

1. Click **Cancel plan** and confirm in the Stripe portal.
2. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. The badge shows **Canceling** and the date reads **Cancels**.
3. After that date the plan ends. **Your remaining credit balance is kept**, but you don't get new monthly credits, and you'll need to choose a plan to keep using the studio.

## Resume

Changed your mind before the cancel date? Click **Resume plan**. Nothing else changes.
