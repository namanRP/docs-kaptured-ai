Kaptured AI plans are credit-based. One credit balance powers everything: images, edits and videos. Every plan comes with a monthly allowance, and when you upgrade the new credits arrive immediately.

## The plans

Prices are in US dollars, per month.

| Plan | Best for | Billed yearly | Billed monthly | Credits every month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Studio** | Solo founders and early brands | $19/mo | $24/mo | 1,000 |
| **Brand** (most popular) | Growing brands shipping weekly | $49/mo | $59/mo | 3,000 |
| **Scale** | High-velocity catalog teams | $119/mo | $145/mo | 8,000 |

Yearly billing saves up to 20%. **On yearly plans you still get your credits every month**, not all at once.

## Standard and Pro quality

In the studio, **Standard** is the **v1** image engine ("Best Value") and **Pro** is **v2** and **v3** ("Most Powerful").

- **Standard** is fast and affordable, great for everyday catalog volume.
- **Pro** gives the most detail and realism, for hero images, campaigns and jewelry close-ups.

You choose per image, so you control the spend. See [Final Settings and Image Engines](/ai-photo-studio/final-settings-and-image-engines).

## What credits buy

As a rough guide, 1,000 credits is about 80 Standard images or 25 Pro images at 1K. See [How Credits Work](/subscription-and-billing/credits-explained) for the full price list.

## Free credits

New accounts can unlock **20 free credits** by booking a short onboarding call. The Free plan is for trying things out:

- Up to 3 products and 3 customers
- Standard (v1) image engine and square (1:1) images only
- No custom model faces, bulk upload, AI Search, Magic Edit, video, enhanced or logo downloads, or Virtual Try-On

## Want us to shoot it for you?

Our **done-for-you studio** is the full-service option. ## Choose a plan

Go to **Billing & Plans**, choose **Yearly** or **Monthly**, and click **Choose {Plan}**. You pay securely through Stripe. When you come back you'll see "Payment complete. Your credits are being applied."

Not sure which plan fits? Click **Talk to sales** on the Billing page to chat with us on WhatsApp.
