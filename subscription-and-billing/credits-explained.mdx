Everything you generate in Kaptured AI is paid for with credits. This article explains what things cost, when your credits refresh, and which credits get used first.

## What things cost

### Images

| Image engine | 1K | 2K | 4K |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **v1** (Best Value) | 12 | 20 | 35 |
| **v2** (Most Powerful) | 40 | 50 | 90 |
| **v3** (Most Powerful) | 40 | 50 | 90 |

Each engine you select in **Final Settings** makes its own image and is charged separately. [Editing an image](/editing-and-video/editing-images) with a prompt or preset costs the same as one image at the engine you pick.

### Videos

| Video model | 4 seconds | 6 seconds | 8 seconds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lite** | 100 | 150 | 210 |
| **Fast** | 120 | 180 | 250 |
| **Omni Flash** | 120 | 180 | 250 |
| **Quality** | 200 | 300 | 400 |

### Free actions

- Cropping
- **Enhance** downloads (they run in your browser)
- Compressed and with-logo downloads
- Adding products and customers

<Note>
  Prices can differ on some accounts. The confirm dialog always shows the exact cost before anything is charged.
</Note>

## When credits refresh

- **Plan credits refresh at the start of every calendar month.** Your full monthly allowance is added, and **unused plan credits from the previous month don't roll over**.
- When you first subscribe, you get your allowance straight away.
- On yearly plans, credits still arrive monthly.

## Top-up credits

Top-ups are added straight away and last **90 days** from purchase. They're not affected by the monthly refresh.

## Which credits are used first

Credits that expire soonest are used first. In practice, top-up credits are usually spent before your plan credits, so they don't go to waste.

## See where your credits went

On **Billing & Plans**, click **Usage history**. **Credit usage history** lists every charge and every credit added. Filter by:

- **Credit type:** All transactions, Credits used, or Credits added
- **Date range:** All time, 7, 30 or 90 days
- A search by reason

## Running out

If you don't have enough credits, the confirm button is disabled and the dialog shows "Need Y more credits to continue". If you run out mid-way you'll see "You're out of credits to generate images. Please upgrade your plan or purchase more credits."

Buy a top-up or upgrade on **Billing & Plans**. Upgrades add the extra credits for the rest of the month straight away.

## Q: Were failed generations charged?

Images that fail to generate aren't charged. If you think you were charged for a failed image, check **Usage history** and contact support with the time.

## Q: Does my own API key reduce the cost?

No. See [Bring Your Own Key](/account-and-security/bring-your-own-key).
